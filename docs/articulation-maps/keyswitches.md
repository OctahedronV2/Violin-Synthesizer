# Octavio 2 keyswitches

Fixed keys, whatever the Octave setting. Latching: a keyswitch stays in force until another one in its group, or until the Articulation / Contact Point parameter is changed. The two groups combine (tremolo sul ponticello: F1 then A#1).

| MIDI note | Name (C4 = 60) | FL Studio name (C5 = 60) | Switches to | Group |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | C1 | C2 | Arco: bowed (normal) | articulation |
| 25 | C#1 | C#2 | Pizzicato: plucked | articulation |
| 26 | D1 | D2 | Bartok pizz: snap pizzicato | articulation |
| 27 | D#1 | D#2 | Left-hand pizz: plucked by a left-hand finger | articulation |
| 28 | E1 | E2 | Harmonics: natural or artificial harmonics | articulation |
| 29 | F1 | F2 | Tremolo: quick strokes in one note | articulation |
| 30 | F#1 | F#2 | Sautille: bouncing strokes, mid-bow | articulation |
| 31 | G1 | G2 | Portato: pulses in one bow | articulation |
| 32 | G#1 | G#2 | Col legno battuto: the stick strikes the string | articulation |
| 33 | A1 | A2 | Ordinario: contact point as dynamics place it | contact point |
| 34 | A#1 | A#2 | Sul ponticello: bowed at the bridge | contact point |
| 35 | B1 | B2 | Sul tasto: bowed over the fingerboard | contact point |
