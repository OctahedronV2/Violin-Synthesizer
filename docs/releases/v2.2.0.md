**Octavio 2.2** adds the Bow and Left hand tabs, MIDI learn and presets, player styles and tunings, and new articulations including a baroque violin.

## See and shape the player

- **Play tab**: live knobs, the current shift and how much bow is left.
- **Bow tab**: the bow's position, speed, force and contact point as they happen. New controls: **Bow Change**, **Stroke Shaping**, **Bite** and **Contact Point**.
- **Left hand tab**: the string and position of every note, the shifts and the vibrato. New controls: **Portamento**, **String Preference**, **Vibrato Rate** and **Vibrato Delay**.

## MIDI and presets

- **MIDI tab**: map any controller to any control with **Learn**, or load a ready map (Octavio 2, Legacy or None). The map is saved with your project.
- **Sustain pedal (CC64) slurs**: while it is down, every note joins the next.
- **Pitch bend** bends the bowed notes. **Bend Range** sets how far (2 semitones by default).
- **Presets**: 15 factory presets in the header. Save your own; they go to Documents/OctahedronV2/Octavio 2/Presets.

## Player styles and tuning

- **Player** (header menu): Modern soloist (the default, as in 2.1), Romantic, Hungarian, Baroque, Maqam, Fiddle and Student. Each changes the player's vibrato, slides, bow strokes and swells, and still follows your controls.
- **Tuning** (Tone tab):
  - **A4** from 415 to 466 Hz.
  - **Intonation**: Expressive, Equal, Just, Pythagorean, a Scala file (.scl with an optional .kbm) or MTS-ESP.
  - **Key** for the Just and Pythagorean systems.
- **MPE** (MIDI tab): per-note pitch bend, pressure for dynamics and CC74 for the contact point (lower zone).

## New articulations and instrument parts

- **Articulations**: Tremolo (free, or synced to the host tempo), Sautillé, Portato and Col legno battuto join Arco, the pizzicatos and Harmonics.
- **Contact** (Articulation tab): Ordinario, Sul ponticello or Sul tasto. It combines with any bowed articulation, for example tremolo sul ponticello.
- **Keyswitches** now run from C1 to B1 (MIDI 24 to 35):

| Key | Picks |
|---|---|
| C1 to E1 | Arco, Pizzicato, Bartók pizz, Left-hand pizz, Harmonics |
| F1, F#1, G1, G#1 | Tremolo, Sautillé, Portato, Col legno battuto |
| A1, A#1, B1 | Ordinario, Sul ponticello, Sul tasto |

- **Articulation maps** for Reaper (Reaticulate), Cubase and Logic are in `docs/articulation-maps/` on GitHub.
- **Tone tab**:
  - **Strings**: Synthetic, Gut or Steel.
  - **Rosin**: Light, Standard, Dark or Baroque.
  - **Bow**: Modern or Baroque.
  - **Instrument**: one click for a **Baroque violin**, which sets gut strings, baroque rosin, the baroque bow, A415, Pythagorean tuning and the Baroque player. **Modern violin** sets them back.

With every new control at its default, Octavio 2.2 sounds exactly like 2.1, and every new control can be automated.

## Install

| Platform | File | Install to |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `Octavio2-VST3-Windows.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Octavio2-VST3-macOS.zip`, `Octavio2-AU-macOS.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` or `.../Components/` |
| Linux | `Octavio2-VST3-Linux.zip` | `~/.vst3/` |

**Updating from 2.1:** close your DAW, then unzip and copy `Octavio 2.vst3` over the old one. Standalone apps are in the `Octavio2-Standalone-*.zip` files. The macOS builds are not notarised; see the README for the one-line `xattr` command that lets them load.

## Known limits

- **Changed from 2.1:**
  - Keys G1 to B1 (MIDI 31 to 35) are now keyswitches. They used to play notes when Octave was +2.
  - Articulation automation drawn in 2.1 may land on different choices, because the list is longer. Saved projects keep their articulation.
- **Loud playing can clip:**
  - Fortissimo double stops can peak a few dB above 0 dBFS, and so can Bartók pizz.
  - Turn Volume down a few dB if you play that hard.
- **Tuning:**
  - Expressive intonation is the same as Equal with the Modern soloist. The other players add their own leanings.
  - MPE supports the lower zone only.
- **Articulations:**
  - Sautillé is convincing only on quick notes.
  - Portato plays a few dB louder than détaché.
  - Col legno tratto and flautando are not included yet.
- **String sets:** the gut and steel values are estimates, not fitted to recordings.
