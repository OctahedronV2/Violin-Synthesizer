**Octavio 2.3** lets you take charge: draw curves inside the plugin, choose who controls each dimension, and pick articulations from the Play tab.

## Draw curves

- **Curves tab**: draw Dynamics, Vibrato width, Vibrato rate and Contact point lanes on your DAW's timeline. Tools:
  - **Draw**: freehand. Click to add a point, drag a point to move it, double-click to remove it, and Shift-drag for a straight line.
  - **Line** and **Erase**.
  - **Right-click menu**: fill, clear, undo and mode. Ctrl+Z also undoes.
- What the player played shows dashed under your curve.
- **Guess curves** fills the lanes with the player's own curves from the last playback so you can edit them.
- **Drag curves as MIDI** still works.
- Curves are saved with your project. Drawing needs a DAW transport, so it is off in the standalone app.

## Auto, Guided or Manual

- **Click any A/G/M badge** to choose who is in charge of Dynamics, Vibrato width, Vibrato rate, Contact point or Bow pressure:
  - **Auto**: the player decides, as before.
  - **Guided**: your curve or controller sets the level, and the player keeps its own swells and vibrato shaping on top.
  - **Manual**: exactly your curve or controller.
- A controller lane (CC1, CC26, CC19, CC74 or CC22) now **guides** its dimension instead of taking it over. CC121, or choosing Auto, gives it back.

## Articulations and bowing

- **Play tab**: the articulation strip works. Pick the bow style, contact point, mute and articulation there. It also lights up when a keyswitch or UACC chooses one.
- **Bow style in Live mode**: Staccato, Martelé and Spiccato now play short strokes even though a Live note's length is unknown, and Détaché gives every note its own stroke. The Play tab shows the stroke that plays.
- **Keyswitches** (MIDI tab):
  - **Behaviour**: Latching (as before), Momentary (only while held) or Off.
  - **Start key**: moves the whole block.
- **UACC** (CC32) picks articulations; the values are in `docs/articulation-maps/README.md`.

## More control

- **Instrument menu** (header): Modern or Baroque violin in one click.
- **Take** (header): roll the dice for a different performance; take 1 is the 2.2 performance.
- **Imperfection** (Tone tab): adds a human's small errors: pitch landings the ear corrects, uneven bow strokes and loose timing.
- **Velocity Sensitivity** and **Attack weight** (MIDI tab): how far velocity moves the dynamics and the bite of the attack.

With every new control at its default, 2.3 sounds exactly like 2.2.

## Install

| Platform | File | Install to |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `Octavio2-VST3-Windows.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Octavio2-VST3-macOS.zip`, `Octavio2-AU-macOS.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` or `.../Components/` |
| Linux | `Octavio2-VST3-Linux.zip` | `~/.vst3/` |

**Updating from 2.2:** close your DAW, then unzip and copy `Octavio 2.vst3` over the old one. Standalone apps are in the `Octavio2-Standalone-*.zip` files. The macOS builds are not notarised; see the README for the one-line `xattr` command that lets them load.

## Known limits

- **Curves:**
  - The bar numbers assume the time signature doesn't change after bar 1.
  - Looping back clears what was recorded after that point, so the tab shows only the latest pass.
  - Bow pressure has no lane yet; CC22 drives it.
- **Curve exports from 2.2:** they recorded the swelled dynamics, so long-note swells come out deeper with Guided playback. A fresh Guess or export avoids this.
- **Spiccato:** the first note from rest is weaker than the ones after it.
- **UACC:** any CC32 value above 0 changes the articulation, including bank select LSB messages.
- **Loud playing:** very loud double stops and Bartók pizz can still peak above 0 dBFS.
