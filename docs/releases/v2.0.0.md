**Octavio 2** is a new violin, rebuilt from scratch on physical models of the string, the bow, the violin body and the room, and played by a virtual violinist who plans each bow stroke. It installs beside Octavio 1, which stays available from the 1.1 release.

## A player, not a patch

- **Live and Studio modes.** Live plays at once, for keyboards. Studio looks 1.2 s ahead (reported as latency, so your DAW lines it up), so the player knows how long each note is and bows, shifts and vibrates the way a violinist reading the part would.
- **Velocity is bow pressure and speed**, across the whole range. The **Curves** tab shows and edits the dynamics, vibrato and bow the player chooses for each note.
- **Natural bow changes.** Strokes ease off just before the bow turns, as real players do.

## Bow physics

- **Rosin that gives before it slides**, so the tone brightens as the bow presses harder.
- **Hair that is stiffer at the frog and tip** than in the middle of the bow.
- **Bow Hiss** (Tone tab): Natural is a real violin's balance; 3x and 6x bring the hair's hiss forward.

## Violin, microphones and room

- **Four violins**: Stoppani, Klimke, Levaggi (all measured) and Iowa (estimated from recordings).
- **Four microphone positions** (front, above, player's ear, side), chosen by clicking them on the drawing, each hearing the violin from its own direction.
- **Movement**: the player's slow sway turns the violin between directions, so the sound stays alive.
- **Distance, Width, Bridge and Mute** (con sordino or practice mute).
- **Eight measured rooms**, from concert halls and a church to studios, or none for your own reverb.

Every control can be automated.

## Install

| Platform | File | Install to |
|---|---|---|
| Windows | `Octavio2-VST3-Windows.zip` | `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\` |
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Octavio2-VST3-macOS.zip`, `Octavio2-AU-macOS.zip` | `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/` or `.../Components/` |
| Linux | `Octavio2-VST3-Linux.zip` | `~/.vst3/` |

Unzip, copy to the folder above, then rescan plugins in your DAW and add **Octavio 2** (vendor OctahedronV2). Standalone apps are in the `Octavio2-Standalone-*.zip` files. The macOS builds are not notarised; see the README for the one-line `xattr` command that lets them load.

Very loud playing (velocity near 127) in the concert hall can peak above 0 dBFS; turn Volume down a few dB if you play that hard.
