Bow on String

One violin string and a bow, heard through a violin body (or bare). While a key is held, the bow moves at a constant speed with constant pressure. Find the settings where the string settles into Helmholtz motion.

Computer keys: Z row = C4, Q row = C5.

Press Start, then a key
String velocity under the bow, three periods. Helmholtz motion: a long flat stretch at the bow's speed (the string sticks to the hair), then one short fast swing back (it slips), once per period.
Faster bow, louder note. A faster bow also tolerates more pressure.
Distance from the bridge, as a share of the string. Nearer the bridge is brighter and needs more pressure.
0%: the bare string's pull on the bridge, a buzzy sawtooth. 100%: the real violin body. Above that, the body's peaks and dips are exaggerated, up to 8 times as deep.
